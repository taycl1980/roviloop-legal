How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Roviloop Pro screen (Profile → Roviloop Pro) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Roviloop, then Cancel Subscription. If you have Pro, Profile → Roviloop Pro opens the same screen. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does Roviloop Pro include?

Every account gets 5 free AI meal scans. Roviloop Pro adds AI meal scans, up to 30 a day. Everything else in the app is free.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Privacy & data → Delete all my data. This erases everything on your phone and on our servers. It doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that separately. See the Privacy Policy.

Are the calorie estimates accurate?

AI scans are estimates from a photo, not medical or dietary advice. You can edit any result before logging it.
