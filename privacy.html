Privacy Policy

Roviloop — Eat smarter. Move more. Build better habits.
Effective 12 October 2026

Roviloop is built to keep your data on your phone. This policy explains the small amount that leaves it, why, and how to delete it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile, goals, meals, weight, water, sleep and workouts.
  • Run routes and GPS locations. Location is used only while a run is recording.
  • Data read from or written to Apple Health. It is never sent to our servers.

You can export this data from Profile → Privacy & data.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • We don’t sell your data.

AI estimates

Meal scans are estimates, not medical or dietary advice. Check with a health professional before making major changes to your diet or exercise.

Deleting your data

Profile → Privacy & data → Delete all my data erases the data on your phone, your server account (with your challenge numbers and scan counts) and your purchase record at RevenueCat. If you are offline, it finishes the next time the app opens.

Deleting your data does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Children

Roviloop is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
